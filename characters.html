Characters

Spoiler-safe for Volume 1. Each profile only tells you what the story has shown by the chapter where the character first appears.

Main cast

Mephesto

The Demon Lord's Apostle

A twenty-one-year-old physics student who fell out of the goddess's light and landed at the foot of the Demon Lord's throne. Offered one power, he asked for the force that keeps galaxies from flying apart.

First seen: Chapter 1

Shiryeon

Commander of the Demon Lord's vanguard

Scarred, amber-eyed and two hundred years a soldier. She laughed in Mephesto's face when he swore he'd marry her one day, and she has not stopped refusing since.

First seen: Chapter 1

Zarvoth Kael-Morr

The Demon Lord

Golden-haired, gold-eyed ruler of the demon lands for more than a thousand years. His enemies were given twenty heroes. He kept the twenty-first for himself.

First seen: Chapter 1

Sereth Vaun

Physician of the lower west tower

A wraith-kin healer the rest of the castle prefers to avoid. Clinical, patient and very interested in what a human body can be made to survive.

First seen: Chapter 5

Skeev

Goblin of the east wing

Forty-seven years in the castle, sweeping halls and hearing everything. Skeev knows many things, and Skeev will happily tell you so.

First seen: Chapter 5

Bris Hollowpot

Kitchen imp

Cook, cleaner, server and the loudest welcome in the castle. She feeds the Apostle first and asks questions with her mouth full of gossip.

First seen: Chapter 3

Thalia Grey

Head of household staff

Crisp, composed and never without her small leather notebook. Even Skeev is wary of that notebook.

First seen: Chapter 6

Seraphiel Auren

The Goddess

Radiant, gentle and adored. She summoned twenty-one students from Lecture Hall 3 to be her heroes. Twenty arrived.

First seen: Chapter 1

Shiori Kanzaki

Classmate, Lecture Hall 3

The student who answered the professor's question about gravity, and the one person who watched the light around Mephesto turn the color of blood.

First seen: Chapter 1

Around the castle

Old Corvane

The castle's bone-mender

An old healer of broken soldiers. When the vanguard comes home hurt, it is Corvane who grumbles over them and binds them.

First seen: Chapter 4

Mordekai

Collector

A greater demon who keeps a den of rare and dangerous things. Everything on his walls has killed someone. Most of it has killed many someones.

First seen: Chapter 11

Character art is official concept art for the series. Some designs show a character as they look later in Volume 1.
